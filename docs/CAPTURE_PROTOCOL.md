# Monthly capture and review guide

Goal: 20–30 tracked moles with a consistent route in less than 60 minutes. This is an unvalidated usability target. Suggested budget: 5 minutes setup, 25 capture, 10 import/coverage, 20 compare/retake. Record actual time and unfinished areas during a real trial; revise the route rather than treating gaps as complete.

## Setup
Use the phone rear camera on a tripod with timer, or help from a trusted family member. Transfer original files by USB/file picker. Use repeatable diffuse lighting, distance, angle and background. Prefer the same phone, lens and camera mode. Disable beauty filters and avoid computational HDR where possible. EXIF is optional, never proof of consistent capture. Changing device/mode/light requires extra caution. No cloud transfer is part of this application.

Use a physical matte multi-patch color reference in the same frame, plane and light as the target. Select a card with trustworthy manufacturer or measured patch data and a documented D50/2° profile. The app does not ship values for any commercial card. A gray patch alone is not full calibration. Keep card surfaces clean; avoid reflection and bending. A card cannot undo arbitrary HDR, local shadows, clipping or glare.

## Repeatable route
1. Head/face/ears/neck, then part hair systematically for scalp; use help or a mirror
2. Front torso, sides, arms and underarms; inspect folds without invasive positioning
3. Upper/lower back and backs of arms; use help, tripod/timer or mirrors
4. Front/back/inner/outer legs and comfortable visible folds
5. Hands: backs, palms, between fingers and nails
6. Feet: tops, soles, heels, between toes and nails
7. Optional externally visible skin–mucosal junctions only when comfortable; never require intimate photos, insertion or invasive capture. Record 'not photographed' when appropriate; seek professional help for concerns or inaccessible areas

Each region gets an overview for location and closeups for detail. Use stable ID and left/right + anatomical landmarks, not only row order. Nearby moles can share a regional photo if every mole has enough sharp, unclipped pixels and the card is valid for all. Do not substitute a wide overview for inadequate closeups. Record gaps explicitly.

## Before moving on
Check focus at full resolution, visible fine detail, even illumination, card patches, mole edges and comparable surrounding skin. Retake blur, glare, local shadow, clipped highlights, insufficient size or missing reference. Mark mole interior and normal skin away from inflammation/tan lines, excluding hairs, reflections and boundary pixels. Skin may change independently: inspect both L* values as well as D. Do not apply normal-skin D to mucosal/nail/non-ordinary-skin observations.

## Interpretation and help
Numbers describe an experimental photographic method, not health or melanoma risk. There is no universal clinical delta threshold. A missing or unchanged number is not reassurance. New, changing, itching or bleeding spots should be discussed with a dermatologist without waiting for the next session.
